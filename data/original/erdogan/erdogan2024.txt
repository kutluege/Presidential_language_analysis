My Beloved Nation,

I greet you all with my heartfelt feelings and affection.

Today, we bid farewell to 2024 and welcome the year 2025 with new hopes, expectations, and dreams.

First and foremost, I pray to Almighty God that the new calendar year will bring goodness to our country, our nation, the broader geography with which we share bonds of heart and culture, and all of humanity.

I remember with mercy all our martyrs who laid down their lives for our independence and our future over the past year, and I express my gratitude to our veterans.

As a nation, God willing, we will further strengthen our unity, solidarity, and togetherness in 2025 and stand even more firmly together.

The year 2024, which we are leaving behind, witnessed many critical developments both within our country and across our region.

We successfully held our Local Administration Elections in an atmosphere of a true celebration of democracy, once again demonstrating to the entire world the maturity of Turkish democracy.

While the will of our nation was freely expressed at the ballot box, once again, alongside our democracy, all 85 million of our citizens emerged as the winners.

Regardless of their preferences, I would once again like to thank all my fellow citizens who turned the ballot boxes into places of celebration.

Despite the elections and the new crises that erupted in our region, we have begun to reap the benefits of the economic program that we have been implementing with determination.

We achieved very important successes in employment, exports, production, tourism, the defense industry, and many other fields.

We made significant progress in our efforts to rebuild and revive our earthquake-stricken regions, and these efforts will accelerate further in the new year.

The continuing decline in inflation, particularly at an increasing pace during the final months of the year, confirms that we will achieve our targets for 2025.

By giving greater priority to policies that will compensate our citizens for their loss of welfare and increase their purchasing power, God willing, we will reach much better conditions.

In 2025, we will continue with determination our fight against opportunists who seek to profit at the expense of our people through exorbitant prices, particularly in housing, rents, and food.

I especially ask my fellow citizens not to give credence to populist rhetoric that has imposed very heavy costs on Türkiye in the past.

Just as we have resolved the problems of this country and this nation over the past 22 years, we will once again overcome the current cyclical difficulties in the economy.

We are on the right path; with God's permission, we will achieve our goals.

All we ask of you is a little more patience, resilience, and understanding.

My Beloved Nation,

The final weeks of 2024 marked the beginning of a new era in Syria, a country with which we share deep-rooted historical, human, and neighborly ties.

We will provide every form of support necessary for this new era to open the way for lasting peace, tranquility, stability, and economic prosperity in Syria.

As stability takes root in Syria, I believe that the voluntary return of Syrian refugees who have longed for their homeland for the past 13 years will also become easier.

I say, “May God be pleased with you,” to all my fellow citizens who, throughout this period, displayed the finest examples of being an “Ansar Nation,” a nation that offers refuge and support.

We are the country that has given one of the strongest responses to the massacres in Gaza from the very first day and has demonstrated its conscientious and just stance on every platform.

We are making intensive efforts to bring an end to the massacres that have continued for 15 months in Gaza and the other Palestinian territories and to establish peace there as well.

In 2025, we will continue to work with all our strength for the establishment of a free, sovereign, and territorially integral Palestinian State based on the 1967 borders, with East Jerusalem as its capital.

Bringing the war between Russia and Ukraine, both of which are our neighbors across the Black Sea, to an end through a just peace will remain one of our priorities.

Our hope is that a new era will begin to our north as well in 2025.

While carrying out all these diplomatic initiatives beyond our borders, our most important agenda at home will be the strengthening of our “home front.”

We are determined to make the Century of Türkiye a century of brotherhood.

In the period ahead, we will take determined steps to turn our vision of a “TERROR-FREE TÜRKİYE” and a “TERROR-FREE REGION” into reality.

We are making every effort to ensure that this process, which will open a new path before our country, proceeds smoothly and with mutual goodwill and understanding.

However, when necessary, we will not hesitate to bring into play the iron fist of our state inside the velvet glove.

Within this framework, we hope and desire, God willing, to bring our nation new good news in 2025.

May Almighty God be our guide and helper.

May Almighty God never let us fall short before our beloved nation, whom we are honored to serve, or before the hundreds of millions of oppressed people who have placed their hopes in Türkiye.

With these wishes, I hope that the new calendar year brings goodness to our country, our nation, and all of humanity, and I greet each and every one of you with respect.

May your new year be blessed.

Stay well and in good health.
