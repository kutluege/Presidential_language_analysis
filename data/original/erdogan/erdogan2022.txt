My Dear Nation,

I greet each and every one of you with my warmest feelings and deepest affection.

Today, we are not only bringing one year to a close and welcoming a new one; we are also entering a period in which we will leave behind the first century of our Republic and step into its new century.

We began the centennial commemorations of the National Struggle in Samsun in 2019.

In fact, we had hoped that, together, we would celebrate with great enthusiasm every centennial anniversary of the events that took place during the four-year period leading up to the proclamation of the Republic.

We had also made very serious preparations for this.

However, the measures implemented within the framework of the COVID-19 pandemic forced us to mark this period with more modest commemorative ceremonies.

So much so that, even on the 100th anniversary of the opening of the Grand National Assembly, we had to make do with messages of congratulations.

Praise be to God, we have left those difficult days behind us.

God willing, we will be able to celebrate the joy of the 100th anniversary of the founding of our Republic on October 29, 2023, with the enthusiasm we had envisioned.

Moreover, 2023 also marks the milestone of a promise we made to our nation 12 years ago.

Since the 2011 elections, when we presented our 2023 goals—one of the most ambitious visions for democracy and development in our country—to the judgment of our nation and received its approval, we have worked day and night to fulfill this promise.

The events that began immediately after Türkiye announced its 2023 goals and have continued without interruption until today are, in one respect, an indication of how many centers of power we unsettled with this step.

From the Gezi events of 2013 to the December 17–25 police-judiciary coup attempt that same year…

From the PKK’s trench operations to DAESH’s harassment of our borders…

From the attempted coup of July 15, 2016, to the international sabotage targeting our economy in 2018…

Throughout this critical period, in which we were subjected to countless attacks from all sides, we never took a step back from our 2023 goals while simultaneously carrying out a multifaceted struggle.

On the contrary;

By strengthening our national unity and solidarity…

By expanding our counterterrorism strategy in the direction of eliminating threats at their source…

By burying terrorists in the trenches they had dug…

By crushing the coup plotters…

By frustrating economic hitmen through our own programs…

By mobilizing our country’s resources at full capacity in order to turn global crises into opportunities…

In short, by struggling together with all our strength, we have always continued on our path under the guidance of our 2023 goals.

Without a doubt, the economic difficulties that began in the final months of 2021 and continued throughout 2022 have troubled all of us.

The negative developments around the world, which struggled to withstand the economic shock caused first by the global pandemic and then by the Russia-Ukraine war, inevitably affected us as well.

However, during this period, the most important feature distinguishing Türkiye from the rest of the world, particularly from developed countries, has been its economic model, which refuses to compromise on employment and production.

Yes, this country has experienced a rise in the cost of living.

But everyone with a conscience will acknowledge that, during the same period, this country also reached the highest employment level, the highest production capacity, and the highest export figures in its history.

In other words, we did not leave our nation alone in the face of the rising cost of living, nor did we abandon our people to their fate.

Instead;

We increased employment so that everyone could earn an income…

We tried to compensate for the loss of welfare experienced by workers through substantial increases…

We continuously supported industry and commerce so that the wheels of the economy would keep turning.

If Türkiye today is still standing tall despite all the attacks it has endured and all the crises that have swept across the world, we owe this to the determined struggle we have waged and to the distinctive political and economic policies we have pursued.

No one can deny the reality of a Türkiye that, through its sensible, composed, peace-oriented and moderate stance in international crises, has earned the respect of everyone, including its longstanding adversaries.

While the world has been grappling with an energy crisis, through our own discoveries and through our position as a center of international supply and trade routes, we have placed ourselves in a different position.

We have progressed from the days when terrorist organizations’ attacks targeting our cities were turned into an instrument of blackmail against our country to a level at which we are able to conduct operations dozens, and sometimes hundreds, of kilometers beyond our borders.

Wherever we travel in our country, we see hundreds of factories under construction, being opened, and shipping goods intensively both domestically and abroad.

In tourism, we have not only compensated for our losses, but are also concluding a season in which we reached the highest level of revenue in our history.

Whichever field you look at, you will see similar pictures.

I would like to underline and repeat once again that we are closing 2022, just like the past 20 years, with gains greater than the difficulties we have faced.

God willing, in 2023, with our Türkiye Century vision, we will launch a much greater breakthrough in our country, one worthy of the new century of our Republic.

Our goal is no longer merely to enter the highest tier of the global political and economic league.

Our goal is to attain the political and economic power necessary to take our place among the very top ranks of that highest tier.

We regard the infrastructure of democracy and development that we have brought to our country over the past 20 years as the foundation, the beginning, the “Bismillah” of this great leap forward.

In other words, we are only just beginning.

Our aim is to make this beginning in the best and most effective way possible and to leave our young people a country in which they can carry the Türkiye Century to success in a manner consistent with our goals, our dreams, and our civilizational and historical heritage.

With these feelings, I bid farewell to 2022 and wish every member of our nation, and all of humanity, a 2023 filled with peace, tranquility, prosperity, and well-being.

May God protect you.

Stay well and in good health…
