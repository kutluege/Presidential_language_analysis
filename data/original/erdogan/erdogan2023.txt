My Dear Nation,

I greet each and every one of you with my most heartfelt feelings and affection.

Tonight, we are bringing the year 2023 to a close and stepping into 2024.

I wish for the new calendar year to bring goodness to our country, our nation, and all of humanity.

In fact, we believe that every new year should be welcomed with joy, hope, and excitement.

However, we are entering this new year with a sense of sorrow, both because of the adverse developments in our region and around the world, and because of the martyrs we lost in recent days.

Of course, we continue to preserve our hope for a better, more peaceful, and more prosperous future for all of humanity.

For this to happen, first of all, we need to see the so-called democratic and freedom-loving countries cease their support for terrorist organizations with blood on their hands.

For this to happen, first of all, we need to see all countries and institutions take a common stance against the killing of innocent children and women in Gaza.

For this to happen, first of all, we need to see fair and sincere efforts being made to bring an end to conflicts, particularly the Russia-Ukraine war, which inflict suffering on individuals and squander the resources of countries.

For this to happen, first of all, we need to see the wealth of societies that have been exploited for centuries and whose dignity has been trampled upon being used for their own future, prosperity, and security.

In short, in order to turn hopes into reality, we need to ensure that all countries, institutions, and individuals around the world unite around common values and principles.

As Türkiye, we represent a different place, a different mission, and a different understanding in this global picture.

As a state and as a nation, we are not merely striving to bring our own efforts for security and prosperity to fruition.

With the understanding that we ourselves cannot find peace unless an atmosphere of peace prevails in our region and throughout the world, we seek the same standards for everyone.

With this understanding,

We are working to bring peace efforts in our region to fruition.

We are developing our relations with our friends in every field.

We share the troubles and concerns of our brothers and sisters.

We support every effort aimed at preparing the world for a better, fairer, and more prosperous future.

At a time when we have completed the first century of our Republic and entered its new century, which we call the Century of Türkiye, we continue to reinforce our determination and efforts as we set our sights on even greater goals.

We believe that the oppression of the oppressor will not continue forever.

We believe that the unjust and unbalanced global system of governance is going through its final struggles.

We believe that the voice of the oppressed is spreading deeply throughout the world, and that these cries will grow and turn into the common conscience of humanity.

Indeed, we see that Türkiye’s statements and positions, which address not only its own citizens, friends, and brothers and sisters but all of humanity, are increasingly finding resonance in people’s hearts.

My Dear Nation,

Of course, along this arduous path, we constantly encounter new trials, new difficulties, and new obstacles.

At the root of many of the problems we face, from the fight against terrorism to economic traps, lies the objective of preventing the construction of a great and powerful Türkiye.

As long as we, as a country, continue to use our potential and capabilities effectively, this struggle will become even tougher.

Because Türkiye’s growth means disrupting the calculations of those who, for centuries, have been able to act freely all around us thanks to our setbacks.

Our becoming stronger means bringing an end to the systems of those who use, exploit, and oppress everyone else for the sake of their own prosperity and security.

For our voice to be heard more means strengthening the pursuit of rights, justice, freedom, and conscience throughout the world.

At no point in its history has our nation shied away from paying a price, making sacrifices, or taking responsibility in order to achieve its goals.

The strong stance our nation has displayed in the face of the many critical events we have experienced over the past 21 years shows that this determination continues today.

Yes, I would like to repeat this once again from here.

As long as our nation protects its unity, solidarity, and brotherhood, with God’s permission, no one will be able to divide us.

As our state implements the Century of Türkiye vision, which is the next stage of our 2023 goals, with God’s permission, our crescent-and-star flag will continue to rise.

As we continue, through our political, economic, military, and diplomatic achievements, to inspire confidence in our friends and fear in our enemies, the fog before us will gradually clear.

In short, as long as we do not compromise our independence or our future, no one will be able to stand in the way of our blessed march.

In the past, we lost a great deal of time, energy, and human life because of tutelary forces, coup plotters, terrorist organizations, and political and social engineering projects imposed upon us as instruments of imperialists.

We have long since had enough of these schemes, and we have no intention of paying such heavy prices again.

We parted ways long ago with those who kept our country occupied with internal struggles and deprived it of its historical legacy and the opportunities it should possess.

This is precisely the meaning of the principle we repeat at every opportunity: “ONE NATION, ONE FLAG, ONE STATE, ONE HOMELAND.”

God willing, 2024 will be a year in which we leave behind the difficult period that began with the coup attempt, grew with the COVID-19 pandemic, and deepened with the conflicts in our region, and instead focus on our goals.

At a time when global crises continue to intensify, we will once again demonstrate our difference and raise the star of a Türkiye that produces, creates employment, grows, and develops.

Yes, the 2023 goals were only the beginning; our real rise begins with the Century of Türkiye and with 2024.

We sincerely believe that, with your support, we will bring this struggle to victory as well.

With these feelings, I once again wish for the new calendar year to bring goodness to every member of our nation and to all of humanity.

I greet you all with love and respect.

Stay well and healthy.
