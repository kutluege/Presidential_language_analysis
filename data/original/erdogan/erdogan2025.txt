My Dear Nation,

I greet you all with my most heartfelt feelings, with affection and respect.

Today, we bid farewell to 2025 and prepare to say “hello” to 2026 with renewed excitement, new hopes, and new goals.

I wish for the new calendar year to bring blessings to our country, our nation, the communities and lands to which we are bound by ties of the heart, and all humanity.

I commemorate with mercy all our heroes who were martyred, whether in our fight against terrorist organizations, drug traffickers, and urban criminals, or in the defense of the security and survival of our state.

On behalf of myself and my nation, I extend my gratitude to each and every one of our veterans, who have paid great sacrifices for the peace and well-being of our 86 million citizens.

The year we have left behind witnessed important developments both in our country and around the world.

Despite the growing atmosphere of war, crises, and tensions in our region, Türkiye, under the leadership of capable and competent cadres, continues its blessed journey with confidence.

We are witnessing the positive results of the economic program we have implemented with determination across a broad range of areas.

While the disinflation process continues, the reserves of our Central Bank are growing stronger, and we are shifting into a higher gear in production, investment, employment, and exports.

From tourism to the defense industry, we continue to break record after record in many fields.

We believe that our “500,000 Social Housing Project,” for which the first draws were held two days ago, will not only enable our low-income citizens to become homeowners under highly favorable conditions, but will also contribute to reducing the excessive increases in housing and rental prices.

This year, we intend to crown the gains we have achieved in the economy with reforms.

With the support of our Parliament, God willing, we will implement step by step the Türkiye Century Reform Program that we announced at our Grand Congress.

My Esteemed Citizens,

Throughout 2025, the first priority of our government was to heal the wounds caused by the February 6 Disaster of the Century.

Although we cannot bring back the lives we lost, we have succeeded in rebuilding our cities with their infrastructure, public squares, green spaces, social facilities, and historical and cultural character.

As we promised, on December 27 in Hatay, with the participation of more than 100,000 of our brothers and sisters, we proudly handed over the key to our 455,000th earthquake home to its rightful beneficiaries.

I wish our new homes and workplaces to be auspicious and beneficial for our earthquake survivors.

I would like to thank all our brothers and sisters who, since February 6, have worked at construction sites around the clock, seven days a week, regardless of the cold of winter or the heat of summer, as well as all our institutions and benefactors who contributed to the construction of these homes.

As for those who, instead of supporting the reconstruction efforts, criticize everything that is done, I invite them to Hatay and our other disaster-stricken cities so that they can see firsthand what our state has accomplished.

As the government and the Alliance, we will continue to keep the promises we have made and, God willing, we will never let our nation down.

We will not leave alone not only our own people, but any of our brothers and sisters who look to our country for support.

The reflections of our political approach, which places rights, justice, and conscience at its core, can be seen throughout our region, particularly in Syria and Gaza.

In neighboring Syria, which attained its freedom through the December 8 Revolution, recovery has accelerated and significant progress toward political stability has been made within a short period of time.

As the atmosphere of peace in Syria has become more firmly established, voluntary returns have also increased, and over the past year 600,000 of our Syrian guests have returned to their homeland.

We believe that a strong Syria that has secured its political unity and territorial integrity will make exceptional contributions both to its region and to the world.

As Türkiye, without distinguishing between Arab, Kurd, Turkmen, Sunni, Shia, or Nusayri, we will provide the new administration with the necessary support for the peace and security of the Syrian people.

In Gaza, the ceasefire achieved with our contribution remains in place thanks to the restraint of our Gazan brothers and sisters, despite all of Israel’s violations.

We are making intensive efforts to ensure that Israel’s attacks come to an end, that the flow of humanitarian aid into Gaza accelerates, and that reconstruction efforts can begin.

Until those responsible for the deaths of 71,000 of our brothers and sisters, most of them children and women, are held accountable before justice, we will neither retreat nor remain silent, and we will never allow the genocide to be forgotten.

We also continue our initiatives to bring the Russia-Ukraine War, which is about to enter its fifth year, to an end as soon as possible through a just and lasting peace.

We are closely monitoring the growing provocations and threats directed against the interests of our country and the Turkish Cypriot people in the Eastern Mediterranean.

I would like to underline once again today that we will never tolerate faits accomplis, opportunistic encroachments, or piracy in our Blue Homeland.

My Dear Nation,

We have passed several important milestones in the Terror-Free Türkiye process, which will open an entirely new chapter not only for our country but also for our region.

As the People’s Alliance, we have always acted constructively throughout this process, focused on solutions, and put not only our hands but our entire bodies under the stone, taking full responsibility.

Our National Solidarity, Brotherhood and Democracy Commission, which has carefully listened to the relevant parties, will, God willing, soon finalize its concluding report, which we believe will give new momentum to the process.

It is our hope that the Commission will continue to act in a spirit of consensus during this final stage as well.

This process, which aims to free our country from a 40-year affliction, must not be sacrificed to the calculations of short-term political interests.

For our part, we will not hesitate to do whatever falls upon us to ensure that the process reaches its intended destination without suffering any setbacks.

Just as we have done for the past 23 years, with nothing but love for our nation and our homeland in our hearts, we will continue to serve all 86 million of our citizens and work selflessly for the prosperity, peace, and well-being of all of Türkiye.

In 2026 as well, instead of merely talking, we will produce results; instead of engaging in polemics, we will take action; and we will continue to let our works and investments speak for us.

May God be our guide and helper.

I thank everyone who supports our Türkiye Century vision.

With these thoughts, I congratulate you on the new calendar year and wish for 2026 to bring blessings to our country, our nation, our region, and all humanity.

Once again, I greet you with affection and respect.

Stay well and in good health…
